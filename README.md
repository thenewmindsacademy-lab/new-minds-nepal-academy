# New Minds Nepal Academy

Role & Objective: Act as an expert UI/UX designer and front-end web developer. Design and build a complete, responsive, mobile-first 5-page website for New Minds Academy (newmindsacademy.org.np), a non-profit organization in Nepal dedicated to youth personal development, mindfulness, self-improvement, and personality uplifting.

Visual Aesthetic & Design System:

Style: Ultra-minimalist, modern, spacious layout with generous whitespace and subtle glassmorphism (frosted glass) effects (backdrop-filter: blur(12px), semi-transparent backgrounds, delicate 1px translucent borders, and soft, diffused shadows).

Color Palette (Eye-Friendly & Calming):

Background Base: Soft warm off-white / alabaster (#F8FAF9) with subtle, slow-moving organic gradient blobs (soft sage and pale mist) behind glass cards so the frosted glass effect stands out naturally.

Glass Surface: Translucent white (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)) with a subtle border (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)).

Primary Accent: Calm Sage Green (#4A7C6F) for primary buttons and key highlights.

Secondary Accent: Warm Muted Terracotta / Sand (#D4A373) for subtle badges and secondary highlights.

Typography Colors: Deep Charcoal (#1F2933) for headings and Muted Slate (#52606D) for body text (strictly avoid harsh pure black #000000).

Typography: Clean, modern sans-serif pairing (e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans or Outfit for headings, Inter for readable body copy).

Global Components:

Navigation Bar: Sticky floating glassmorphic header with the New Minds Academy logo on the left, links to the 5 pages (Home, About, Programs, Get Involved, Contact), and a pill-shaped "Join a Program" CTA button on the right. Collapses into a clean glass drawer on mobile.

Footer: Minimalist footer containing quick navigation links, official social media icons (Instagram, TikTok), contact email, operational area (Gokarneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal), and a placeholder badge for NGO registration details.

Page-by-Page Sitemap & Content Structure:

1. Home Page

Hero Section: Centered, spacious layout over a subtle ambient gradient. Headline: "Empowering Youth Through Mindfulness & Personal Growth." Subheadline: "Equipping young minds in Nepal with emotional clarity, confidence, and leadership skills for life." Two CTAs: Primary "Join a Program" and secondary glass button "Partner With Us".

Mission Snapshot: A wide, centered frosted-glass card summarizing New Minds Academy's purpose in 2–3 concise sentences.

Core Program Pillars (3-Column Glass Grid):

Mindfulness & Mental Clarity (Stress management, focus, emotional balance)

Confidence & Communication (Public speaking, self-expression, interpersonal skills)

Leadership & Purpose (Goal setting, resilience, youth empowerment)

Upcoming Pilot Highlight: A sleek glass banner showcasing the next upcoming youth workshop in Gokarneshwor Municipality with date, location, and a "Reserve Your Spot" button.

2. About Page

Our Story & Vision: Clean split layout with a warm visual placeholder on one side and the founding story of New Minds Academy on the other, explaining why youth personal development matters today.

Core Values (4 Minimal Glass Cards): Empathy, Growth Mindset, Authenticity, Community Impact.

Leadership & Team Grid: Minimal profile cards with circular photo frames, names, roles (e.g., Founders, IT & Operations, Program Facilitators), and 1-line bios.

3. Programs Page

Program Tracks (Tabbed or Stacked Glass Cards):

School & College Workshops (1-day interactive sessions on focus and confidence)

Youth Mindfulness Bootcamps (Multi-week personal transformation cohorts)

Personality Uplifting Seminars (Communication, emotional intelligence, and career readiness)

Key Learning Outcomes: A clean checklist-style grid showing tangible skills participants gain.

Schedule / Event List: Minimal table or card list of upcoming sessions with status badges (Open for Registration, School Partnership).

4. Get Involved Page

3-Pathway Glass Cards:

For Youth & Students: Sign up for upcoming workshops.

For Schools & Colleges: Request a workshop at your institution.

For Volunteers & Mentors: Join the movement as a facilitator or organizer.

Embedded Registration Section: Clean glass container styled to seamlessly house an embedded Google Form (include a responsive iframe placeholder) for easy sign-ups.

5. Contact Page

Two-Column Glass Layout:

Left Column: Direct contact details (Official Email, WhatsApp/Phone button, Location: Gokarneshwor Municipality, Kathmandu), plus prominent glass social cards linking to Instagram and TikTok.

Right Column: Minimal contact form (Name, Email, I am a: Student / School / Partner, Message, and Send Message button) + a styled placeholder for an embedded Google Maps / Google Business Profile widget below.

Technical Requirements:

Ensure high contrast ratios for accessibility while maintaining soft, eye-friendly tones.

Smooth, subtle micro-interactions (gentle hover lift on glass cards, smooth scroll).

Semantic HTML5, clean modular CSS (or Tailwind CSS), and fast loading performance.

How to Use This Prompt Effectively

generate the multi-page project using HTML + Tailwind CSS (which handles glassmorphism classes like backdrop-blur-md bg-white/60 border-white/80 effortlessly)
